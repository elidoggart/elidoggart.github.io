    A few ways in Playing & patching

    01 / Play

    Start audio, then touch the striped note pads. A–K plays the eight pads on your keyboard. Sequence repeats them; the small pitch controls change each step. The starter patch connects six voices through an envelope, low-pass gate and tape delay.

    02 / Connect

    Click an output socket, then an input. Outputs have a filled centre; control voltages use red sockets. Connect øchd or Wogglebug to pitch, FM or a gate for motion. Clutch scales a signal; Propust filters it. Remove a cable by clicking its curve or using the connection list.

    03 / Make grains

    Load a short audio file in Morphagene. Gene size, Slide, Varispeed and Morph control overlapping grains. Without a sample, the input passes through a short tape-style delay. Save patch keeps the wiring, settings and notes; reload your audio file separately.

    An independent browser study of timcox’s Sylvan Esso rack, with patching inspired by Bizzmotron Z-100. Original code; no hardware firmware or Bizzmotron source is included. These are simplified sound models, not exact emulations. Propust is represented by one adjustable filter, and Hendrikson by the output stage. No microphone access is needed.
