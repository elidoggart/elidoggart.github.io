BU-1 · Source & credits

BU-1 is an independent browser synthesizer and four-track recording studio.

Sound and recording

The six-voice FM bell and detuned saw adapt open-source DSP by pcppcp, with browser envelopes and output processing. Pure tone, percussion, sampling, effects, modulation, sequencing and tape are browser implementations. BU-1 does not claim bit-exact hardware emulation.

Tape provides six minutes of mono audio per track, overdubbing, editing, loop markers, speed changes and reverse. The mixer exports stereo WAV files. Synth samples support six seconds; imported drum audio supports twelve seconds and 24 editable slices. Lift/Drop copies slice settings between keys. Tape remains in the page session: save a tape project before reloading. Saved sound snapshots and Album sides persist locally in browser storage.

Bend and Crank provide manual modulation; Tremolo supports signed pitch and volume depths. Loop playback and exports use a five-millisecond seam blend, and track mutes ramp over five milliseconds. These are independent DSP choices. Browser audio input requires microphone permission. Radio reception and hardware USB device modes are unavailable.
