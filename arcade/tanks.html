     WATCH THE REBOUND

    Clear the field.

    Destroy every enemy to advance through 100 fields. You start with three tanks and earn another after every five fields. One hit costs a tank. Retrying resets the current field and its unbanked points.

    Controls

    WASD move · Mouse aim · Click / F fire · Space lay a mine · P / Escape pause.

    On touch screens, drag the left pad to move and the right pad to aim and fire. Tap Mine to lay a mine. Your tank turns before moving and can reverse. Firing and mine placement briefly stop it.

    Plan your shot

    Ordinary shells bounce once. Shells cancel each other. You can have five shells and two mines active at once. Pits stop tanks but let shells pass; red-hatched cover can be destroyed by mines. Move away from mines before they detonate, and watch for blast chains.

    Sound

    Music and effects start off. Click either control to toggle it. Drag up or down, or focus the button and use the arrow keys, to adjust its volume.
