Contact submissions

The contact form lets you send Eli Doggart an optional name, email address, message, and file. All fields are optional. Attachments may be any file type, up to 50 MB.

How submissions are stored

Cloudflare processes the form submission and transfers it to Eli’s Google Drive. Each submission is stored in a private folder containing the attachment, if any, and a text file with the entered details. Eli receives an email notification containing the submitted name and a private link to the submission folder. Submissions are not displayed on this website.

Eli uses submissions to review what you send and, when appropriate, reply using the contact details you provide. The form does not subscribe you to a mailing list. Cloudflare and Google may process technical information needed to operate their services.

Google Drive connection

Visitors do not sign in to Google or grant access to their own Drive. Eli authorizes the website backend to access the selected upload folder and files created by the integration. The Google authorization credentials are stored as encrypted backend secrets and are not included in the public website.

Retention and removal

There is no automatic deletion schedule. To ask about a submission or request its removal, email elidoggart@gmail.com with enough information to identify it.
