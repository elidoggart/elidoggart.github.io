A little studio.

Play the computer keys shown below the device. Hold Shift on your computer, or toggle the device Shift key, for secondary functions. All additional settings stay below the device.

Sounds & modules

Synth and Drum have separate sound slots and sequencer memories. The numbered 1–8 keys choose sounds in those modes. T1 is the engine, T2 the envelope, T3 the effect and T4 the LFO. Press T3 or T4 again to toggle that module. Shift + T1 opens the engine selector. The blue, green, white and orange knobs control the four parameters shown below the device.

Sequence a part

Shift + Sequencer opens the sequencer type selector. Select Pattern for a 16-step drum grid, or Endless to enter up to 128 steps. On the sequence screen hold Shift and press notes. Pattern adds/removes notes at the cursor; arrows move the cursor. Endless advances when all notes of a chord are released; Shift + Right adds a rest or extends held notes, Shift + Left deletes the last step. Release Shift and play C4 for original pitch. Orange enables sequence Hold; the other knobs control cursor/rate, swing and length/gate. Drum and Synth retain separate sequences.

Record & arrange

Select your sound, enter Tape, choose T1–T4. REC arms the tape; PLAY or a musical key starts recording. Press REC during playback to punch in/out. Stop halts in place. In Tape, arrows seek; Shift + arrows move one bar. The external Start button rewinds to zero. Blue scrubs, white changes tape speed, orange sets record level. Tape is six minutes and always overdubs.

The 1–8 sound buttons now become tape functions: IN, OUT, Loop, Brake, Reverse, Chop and two memory recalls. Shift + Loop selects the take beneath the head. Scissors splits; Shift + scissors joins. Lift removes the selected take into a clipboard; Drop pastes it at the head and advances to its end. Shift + Lift lifts the same region across all four tracks. Lift loop region uses the loop boundaries. Drop in the Synth sampler resamples the lifted audio (up to six seconds).

Mix & keep your song

Mixer T1 uses the knobs for track levels; Shift adjusts their pan. T2 is EQ, T3 the master delay effect, and T4 master output/drive. Export WAV renders a stereo mix of the loop, or all recorded material when looping is off. Tape and sample data are session-only: export before leaving or reloading.

The tested workflows follow the original OP-1 guide. This is still an independent recreation: the stock firmware, factory sound engines/kits, exact DSP, external hardware inputs and some performance functions are not reproduced. Source and limitations.
