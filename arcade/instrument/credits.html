Source & credits

An independent browser instrument inspired by the layout and workflow of the original Teenage Engineering OP-1. No manufacturer logo, firmware, factory samples or original display artwork is included. This project is not affiliated with Teenage Engineering.

What is emulated?

The six-voice FM bell and detuned saw adapt the DSP algorithms of op1r, custom firmware by pcppcp that runs on the original hardware. They are custom engines, not reproductions of the stock engines. The browser port retains their phase arithmetic, parameter ranges, FM feedback and saw morph/drive structure, while providing its own envelope and output path. It is not bit-exact hardware emulation.

Pure tone, synthesized percussion, sample playback, Delay, Punch-style resonant filtering, Spring-style reverb, CWO frequency-shifting delay, Grid feedback network, Nitro dual filter, Phone band-limiting/quantization, Tremolo/Value/Random modulation, Pattern/Endless sequencers and four-track tape are new browser implementations. Tape provides six minutes of mono audio per track, with overdubbing, take editing, loop markers, speed changes and reverse. The mixer exports stereo WAV audio with level, pan, EQ and master processing. Synth samples are limited to six seconds. Audio stays in this page session; export before reloading.

This is a workflow recreation, not the original firmware. Factory sound engines/kits, exact effects, drum sample slicing, external inputs, radio, USB modes, all sequencer types and some secondary performance functions are not reproduced. Sequencer ties currently follow forward playback. The on-device geometry follows the original instrument, while supplementary controls remain outside it.

Module controls follow the original parameter reference and LFO guide. The two sound-design studies adapt the manufacturer’s helicopter and bird exercises, substituting a noise source/custom FM engine and omitting Tombola/portamento. Effect algorithms, LFO curves and ranges are approximations; Element/MIDI/Crank/Bend LFOs are not implemented. All seven original effect names are available, but their DSP is independently implemented rather than firmware-exact. CWO uses a windowed Hilbert transform for sideband shifting; its animated cow is newly drawn canvas artwork.

Projects used

  • op1r source a2820a7, Copyright © 2026 pcppcp. GPL-3.0-or-later. Original notices. Original engine files: fmbell.c and supersaw.c.
  • op1kenobi by Alex Ownejazayeri, with Shunya Koide’s HTML/CSS design, provides the 34-column, six-row panel geometry adapted here. The arcade treatment, functional controls and display graphics are newly drawn in HTML/CSS/canvas. MIT notices. Its MP3 kit is not used.
  • op1emu was evaluated as a hardware-emulation route. Its native Blackfin emulator is not included in this browser implementation.
