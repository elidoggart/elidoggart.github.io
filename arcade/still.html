     A MOMENT TO THINK

    Stop. Look. Move.

    Clear 12 rooms of red enemies. You have three lives. A bullet or a rushing enemy takes one life. Retry the room; its enemies and items reset, but both clocks keep the time spent on failed attempts.

    Control time

    Standing still slows the world to 2.5% speed. Moving runs it at full speed. Looking advances it a little; firing, throwing, and striking briefly advance it too. Reloading deliberately advances time. Pausing stops everything.

    Find your angle

    WASD move · Mouse look · ← → turn · ↑ ↓ aim up/down
    Click / F fire · R reload · Q / right-click throw held item · E grab / grab a nearby object, or strike an enemy · P / Escape pause

    Click the arena to capture your mouse. If capture is unavailable, drag to look or use the arrow keys. On touch screens, use the two pads and action buttons.

    Read the room

    Red lines are incoming bullets. A dotted aiming line means an enemy is about to fire at your current position. Step sideways. Furniture and walls stop bullets; low cover may leave your head exposed. Red arrows at the edges warn of enemies outside your view.

    Start empty-handed. Look toward a nearby object and press E to grab it. Pistols have three-round magazines and limited spare ammo; R reloads only when you have a spare. Throw bottles, mugs, staplers, or your empty gun to shatter enemies. Enemies drop usable guns. E swaps your held item for a nearby pickup, or strikes within two metres when no pickup is in reach. No weapons or ammo refill automatically.

    Make it count

    Each cleared room starts from 10,000 points, divided by 1 + (world seconds + 0.15 × real seconds) / 10, then multiplied by difficulty. Less time on either clock means more points. World time matters most; real time rewards quick decisions. Pauses, menus, and room-clear screens stop both clocks. Failed attempts count. There are no kill, headshot, or near-miss bonuses. Easy ×1, Medium ×1.5, Hard ×2.5. The HUD shows the current room value; points bank when you clear it. These time scores use a fresh leaderboard.

    Sound

    Effects and music start muted, just like the other games. Click to enable; drag either button up/down or use its arrow keys to adjust volume. The original electronic score adds bass, percussion, and arpeggios when you move, and quiets when you stop.
